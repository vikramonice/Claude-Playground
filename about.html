I hold a NERC Independent Research Fellowship position as Scientist - D in the Antarctic Cryospheric Studies Group at the National Centre for Polar & Ocean Research in Goa, India. My research centers on understanding the interaction between ice rises, ice shelves, and the surrounding ocean in Antarctica, with a particular focus on Dronning Maud Land.

I sit on the steering groups of the SCAR RINGS Action Group and serve as National Representative to the SCAR GeoSciences Group. I recently led the geophysical survey component of the multinational RAICA-CP field expedition on Canisteo Peninsula ice rise in West Antarctica, and the collaborative India-Norway SENS project on Nivl Ice Shelf.

I completed my PhD - titled 'Current status and past changes of ice rises in Dronning Maud Land, Antarctica' - with Kenichi Matsuoka at the Norwegian Polar Institute and Jon Ove Hagen at the University of Oslo in June 2019. Previously, I was a Research Fellow at the University of Edinburgh, and a Postdoctoral Scholar at the Norwegian Polar Institute. My background is in Earth System Science & Technology: I achieved a Distinction in Part III Mathematics from IIT Kharagpur in 2013 and a First Class Honours in Aerospace Engineering from the University of Petroleum and Energy Studies in 2011.

RESEARCH INTERESTS

Antarctic Glaciology, Geophysical data collection and analysis (including ground-penetrating radar and GNSS systems), Ice-flow modelling, Geographical Information Systems (GIS).
