PROJECTS

SUB-SHELF EXPLORATION OF NIVL ICE SHELF (SENS)

2024-PRESENT; COLLABORATIVE PROJECT (INDIA-NORWAY)

Led the collaborative project as Principal Investigator. During the successful SENS expedition on the Nivl Ice Shelf, the team conducted 130 km+ of seismic surveys using novel techniques and 500 km+ of radar surveys to understand ice-ocean interactions beneath the ice shelf.

RAICA-CP: CANISTEO PENINSULA ICE RISE

2023-2024; MULTINATIONAL COLLABORATION (US-S.KOREA-INDIA-NORWAY-UK)

Led the geophysical survey component of this field expedition onboard the ice breaker RV/Araon. The team camped on Canisteo Peninsula ice rise in West Antarctica and drilled two 150-meter ice cores, complemented by 300 km+ of ground-based radar surveys.

INDIAN SCIENTIFIC EXPEDITIONS TO ANTARCTICA

2018-PRESENT

Participated in multiple Indian Antarctic expeditions (38th and 41st), conducting extensive geophysical surveys including 500 km+ deep-sounding radar surveys over Kamelryggen and Verbljud Ice Rises, and collecting various geophysical data on Nivlisen Ice Shelf and Leningrad Ice Rise.
