About

Research Interests

My research focuses on understanding Antarctic ice dynamics and climate history through advanced geophysical techniques and ice-flow modeling.

Antarctic Glaciology

Investigating ice rises and ice shelves in Dronning Maud Land and beyond

Geophysical Analysis

Ground-penetrating radar and GNSS systems for ice structure analysis

Ice-Flow Modelling

Simulating past and present ice dynamics to understand climate change

GIS Applications

Geographic Information Systems for spatial analysis of polar regions
